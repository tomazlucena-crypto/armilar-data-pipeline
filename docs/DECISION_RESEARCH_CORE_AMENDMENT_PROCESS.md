# Decision proposal: Research Core constitutional amendment process

## Status

`PROPOSED`, awaiting explicit human approval.

## Change classes

- `EDITORIAL_PATCH`: no numerical, semantic or eligibility change.
- `RESEARCH_MINOR`: backward-compatible operational or interface change.
- `CONSTITUTIONAL_MAJOR`: any change to basket, economies, categories, weights, base, formula, conceptual treatment or series semantics.

## Mandatory process

Every amendment requires:

1. a dated decision record;
2. affected versions and files;
3. alternatives and rationale;
4. impact analysis;
5. backtest for any numerical change;
6. updated schemas and tests;
7. canonical hashes;
8. explicit human approval;
9. preservation of all previous versions.

Release gates default to false after a constitutional amendment and must be approved separately. Emergency authority is limited to freezing publication or operation. It cannot rewrite history or change the formula.
